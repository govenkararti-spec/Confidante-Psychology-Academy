How Many Attempts Are Allowed in UGC NET? A Complete Guide
Definition
The UGC NET exam currently allows unlimited attempts with no upper limit on how many times a candidate can take the test. However, certain age-related restrictions and eligibility criteria apply based on the specific subject and position you're targeting for academic or research roles.
Introduction
If you're preparing for the UGC NET (National Eligibility Test), one of the most common questions is: "How many times can I appear for this exam?" The good news is that there's flexibility, but understanding the complete rules is essential for planning your exam strategy.
The NET exam has undergone several changes in its policies over the years. Unlike some competitive exams with strict attempt limits, UGC NET offers candidates multiple opportunities to qualify. But this doesn't mean you should take the exam casually knowing the exact rules can help you prepare better and manage your career timeline effectively.
How Many Attempts Are Allowed in UGC NET?
Currently, there is no specified upper limit on the number of attempts you can take for UGC NET. This means you can appear for the exam multiple times until you qualify.
However, this flexibility comes with important conditions:
Age Restrictions Apply
For Junior Research Fellowship (JRF): You must be under 28 years at the time of your first attempt
For Lectureship (LS): There's typically no upper age limit
Age relaxation is available for candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)

No Yearly Attempt Restrictions
The UGC doesn't specify a maximum number of attempts per calendar year. Candidates can take the exam multiple times in a single year if it's conducted multiple times.
